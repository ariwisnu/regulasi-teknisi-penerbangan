Regulasi surveillance penerbangan — kode SSR Mode-S (SAC/SIC/II), ICAO 24-bit address, ELT 406 MHz,
serta spesifikasi & pemeliharaan fasilitas surveillance (PSR/MSSR/ADS-B/MLAT).

## Regulasi

| Nomor | Judul | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [PR 6 DJPU 2024](/surveillance/pr-6-djpu-2024/) | Alokasi SAC/SIC/II Code, ICAO 24-bit, ELT 406 MHz, Location Indicator | **aktif** |
| [KP 243/2014](/surveillance/kp-243-2014/) | ~~Izin Kode SSR Mode-S & ELT 406 MHz~~ | 🚫 dicabut |

## Spesifikasi & pemeliharaan surveillance

Spesifikasi teknis surveillance (PSR, MSSR Mode-S, ADS-B, MLAT, ATC Automation) ada di
[KP 103/2015 (MOS 171-02)](/umum/kp-103-2015/); persyaratan kinerja + pemeliharaan di
[PR-DJPU 08/2025 (MOS 172-03)](/umum/pr-djpu-8-2025/) BAB II. Lisensi/rating: Part 69
([PR 18/2023](/lisensi-rating/pr-18-2023-mos-69-02/)).
